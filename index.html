Selected work

Projects that show the kind of builder I am: systems-minded, metric-driven, and happiest when the pieces fit cleanly together.

Research

Zeroth-order fine-tuning

Built benchmarking and training infrastructure for RoBERTa-large and OPT-family models, with an emphasis on memory efficiency, stability, query efficiency, and reproducible sweeps.

  • Benchmarked fine-tuning without backpropagation.
  • Built resumable studies with guard rails and structured artifacts.
  • Compared trade-offs across transformer families.
  • PyTorch
  • Transformers
  • SLURM
  • Optuna

Systems

Edge-cloud collaborative inference

Implemented a SPINN-inspired pipeline with early exits, split-point profiling, and offloading analysis on Jetson Orin Nano and cloud GPUs.

  • Added early-exit branches and confidence-aware routing.
  • Profiled latency, energy, and feature-map sizes.
  • Kept the implementation close to the research shape.
  • PyTorch
  • Jetson
  • Profiling
  • Edge compute

Deployment

Robustness evaluation and quantization

Transfer-learned RDNet-Tiny on CIFAR-10, applied INT8 quantization, and tested adversarial robustness across multiple attack families.

  • Tracked accuracy, model-size, and inference-speed trade-offs.
  • Used Foolbox attacks to probe failure modes.
  • Focused on efficient deployment, not just benchmark numbers.
  • ONNX Runtime
  • Foolbox
  • Quantization
  • CIFAR-10

Industry

5G traffic prediction

Built forecasting pipelines for historical network traffic with SARIMA, XGBoost, LSTM, and Temporal Fusion Transformer experiments.

  • Prepared compressed, model-ready datasets.
  • Compared classical and deep forecasting approaches.
  • Kept the work focused on practical signal, not noise.
  • SARIMA
  • XGBoost
  • LSTM
  • TFT

How I like to build

The portfolio itself follows the same logic as the work I enjoy: a strong base, small reusable parts, and a result that still feels distinctive.

Modular

Pieces that fit together.

I like systems that can be rearranged without breaking the rest of the build. Good interfaces make it easier to extend a project later.

Measured

Numbers that survive reruns.

I care about experiments that are reproducible and comparable, not just one-off wins that disappear once the environment changes.

Distinctive

A surface that feels like mine.

Blue-gray tones, Lego-like blocks, and sharp hierarchy help the site stand out while still matching the work it represents.
